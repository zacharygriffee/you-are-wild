# You Are Wild 0.19.0

Status: **released select-group alpha**

Channel: **alpha — publicly accessible, intentionally unadvertised**

Release date: **2026-08-13**

This release adds per-companion autonomy control, Combat Agency V1, Companion
Bond V1, finite-mass Chew nourishment, and reach-aware player flight while
preserving Save Schema 11 and Module API 1.

## Added

- Every recruited non-player companion card has an individual Play/Pause
  autonomy control during the player's turn, with active, paused, and
  awaiting-direction states. The player character never receives it.
- Combat Agency V1 previews one autonomous companion's uncommitted preferred
  action and up to three already-ranked legal alternatives.
- The player may suggest one stored alternative. Companion Bond V1 uses a
  bounded saved ledger, recruitment continuity, and authored care events to
  decide compliance or refusal deterministically.

## Changed

- A suggestion reserves the player's next ordinary actionable combat turn
  whether accepted or refused. Forced status skips resolve first and cannot
  erase or double-charge the reservation.
- Chew grants immediate nourishment to participating chewers from the finite
  body mass actually removed from the target. Group Chew divides that conserved
  transfer by deterministic damage contribution.
- Companion autonomy pause, Bond, and pending player-turn reservation state
  survive compatible saves without changing stable identity, history, Duty,
  Stance, or Control.

## Fixed

- Companion flight narrates its authored cause immediately and keeps the party,
  queue, world placement, interaction selection, and saved state consistent.
- A flying player under Terror does not auto-flee solely because of ground-only
  attackers with no legal attack path. Flying, ranged, anti-flying, or otherwise
  reachable attackers retain the existing forced-flight danger.
- Automatic encounters with no legal progress resolve as explicit
  stalemate/disengagement without assigning an unexplained flee flag.

## Compatibility

- Save Schema remains `11`; Module API remains `1`.
- Existing 0.10.x through 0.18.3 saves remain within maintained additive
  normalization. Missing autonomy, Bond, reservation, and finite-mass fields
  initialize deterministically.
- Existing module permissions and minimum-version declarations remain valid.
  This release adds no new module callback or capability.

## Known issues and deliberate exclusions

- This select-group alpha is publicly accessible but intentionally
  unadvertised; broader promotion remains deferred.
- Combat Agency V1 charges a future ordinary turn; true reaction priority and
  emergency out-of-turn player Flee remain deferred.
- Companion Bond V1 does not yet implement loyalty decay, needs, neglect,
  witnessed conduct, desertion, or hostility.
- Physical-device comfort and human review of English and Spanish copy remain
  operator observations.
- Provider-backed companion control and narration remain optional. Deterministic
  play does not depend on a remote endpoint.
